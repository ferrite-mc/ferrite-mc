# Mob AI parity audit (2026-11-14)

Scope: the nine mobs named in the Entities & AI lane — zombie, skeleton, spider, creeper,
enderman, chicken, cow, pig and sheep. A 2026-11-19 breadth slice extends the registry to 39
kinds; the shared behaviour and remaining special goals are summarised below. This is an audit,
not a claim of full parity. The
**evaluated** table lists behaviour that exists and is pinned by a test. The **not evaluated**
table lists vanilla goals that are absent or only approximated; those are the next work.

Vanilla evidence comes from the 26.3 server jar (`vanilla/26.3/versions/26.3/server-26.3.jar`,
official names visible through `javap`) and the generated entity data. Ferrite evidence is the
source path plus the test name. The jar is local and gitignored; it is not distributed.

## Breadth slice (2026-11-19)

`MobKind` now has 39 kinds. The added set is Drowned, Husk, Stray, Bogged, Wither Skeleton,
Blaze, Cave Spider, Witch, Piglin, Zombified Piglin, Iron Golem, Snow Golem, Goat, Fox, Rabbit,
Frog, Turtle, Bee and Mooshroom. Shared family helpers cover zombie/skeleton/spider behaviour,
daylight burning and helmets, bow vs. melee selection, spider climbing/leaping,
zombified-piglin pack alerts, zombie/husk drowning conversion, biome-aware sheep colours, and
the generic animal/breed/tempt goals. The biome spawn parser admits each kind where its vanilla
category is `creature` or `monster`.

## Fixed in Mob Navigation (2026-11-19)

- **Long detours & tick-budget bound (`path.rs`, `ai.rs`)**: Replaced the 16-waypoint / 512-expansion unweighted cap and 32-block `ai::follow_path_with` range with weighted A* (`f = g + 1.5 * h` matching vanilla `PathFinder.FUDGING`), single-map + cached `eval_cell` / sky lookups, `MAX_PATH = 128`, `node_budget(max_range) = (max_range * 96).clamp(1024, 4096)` (`MAX_NODES = 4096`), `mob_path_range` (48 for villagers), `PathOptions::tall` for mobs taller than 2.0 blocks, and `REPATH_TICKS` throttling on empty unreachable paths so worst-case searches do not rerun every tick. Tradeoff: caps CPU per path search to `node_budget` without maintaining a cross-tick chunk evaluator cache or returning partial paths on exhaustion.
- **Region-owned doors & water (`path.rs`, `mob.rs`, `ai.rs`, `region.rs`)**: Extended traversable doors to `#minecraft:mob_interactable_doors` (wooden + copper doors, excluding `iron_door`), blocked diagonal corner cuts on closed doors, wired `InteractWithDoor` (`MobEvent::SetDoor`, `MobState::opened_door`, `path::door_to_open_on_path`, `path::check_door_to_close`) for villagers and piglins using same-region local mob checks, guarded `Region::set_door_open` and `Region::break_door` on `held_chunks` before reading/mutating blocks, and added `GroundPathNavigation.getSurfaceY` upward water scanning (`MAX_SURFACE_SCAN = 16`) for submerged floaters.
- **Spider vertical & overhang pathing (`path.rs`, `ai.rs`)**: Enabled mid-air cardinal wall-face sidestepping (`dy <= 0`, `CLIMB_STEP` cost) and ledge step-offs onto wall faces so spiders climb up, traverse around partial overhangs, and climb down sheer walls taller than `MAX_DROP = 3`, with wall-hugging steering in `ai::follow_path_with`.
- **Skeleton `avoid_sun` & split/merge (`ai.rs`, `region.rs`, `sun.rs`)**: Verified `avoid_sun` detours through shaded corridors in `ai::tick_full` and verified mob navigation state (`path`, `opened_door`, `door_pos`, `shade_pos`) and crossing-based door ownership across `Region::split` / `Region::merge` and `take_crossing`.
